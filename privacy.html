Privacy Policy

SmartTech store and app — Azaz, Aleppo, Syria · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who we are

SmartTech sells genuine Anker, soundcore and eufy products, wholesale and retail. The app is a wrapper around the store website; this policy covers both.

Data we collect

There are no customer accounts or passwords. We collect only what you type when you place an order:

  • Name and phone number
  • Governorate and delivery address
  • Email address and an order note (both optional)

Your cart, favourites and language choice are stored on your own device and are never sent to us.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and communicate about your order.
  • To record order status and issue a receipt link that only you hold.
  • To handle warranty and returns.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with marketers.

Payment

Payment is cash on delivery. We do not collect card or bank details.

Processors

The site is hosted and orders are stored with Vercel, over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. If you contact us on WhatsApp, that chat is governed by WhatsApp's policy.

Retention and your rights

We keep an order as long as accounting and warranty require. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time via WhatsApp: +963 982 760 670. We will act within a reasonable time unless the law requires otherwise.

Children

The store is intended for adults; we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the new date will appear at the top.
